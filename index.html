Renovations and construction. A house goes up in layers, so does our work.

Almost 25 years on Spanish building sites taught us one thing: every layer has to be right before the next one goes on. Here is what we do, in the order it gets built.

Structurefrom the plot up

Design and permits

We start with the plot and the paperwork: survey, project, licence from the town hall. You know what will be built and what it involves before anyone starts digging.

Turnkey construction

Houses and commercial buildings from the foundations to the keys: structure, walls, roof, installations and finishes. One contract, one site manager, one phone number.

Envelopewhat keeps the weather out

Facades

Repair and renewal of facades on houses and apartment blocks: scaffolding, render, insulation and paint, organised so that residents can keep living in the building.

Windows and doors

New windows, balcony doors and entrance doors, fitted with the reveals and sills finished properly after the old frames come out.

Insidewhere you actually live

Renovations

Kitchens, bathrooms and whole flats, including the demolition, the rubble and the mess we take away at the end.

Plumbing and electrics

New and renewed installations to current regulations, laid before the walls are closed, never as an afterthought.

Interiors and finishes

Tiles, plaster, lighting, stairs, decoration. If you want a designer, our interior design partner works with us on site.

And the rest

Small jobs between the big ones: a terrace, a pool repair, a new arch over the kitchen. Ask, most likely we do it.
